# Organic loop

One licensed fight. Complete the swing. Sheathe when the interval is named.

Do not ask at each bubble. Do not invent a `?` to stay alive.

A cycle that does not know when to stop becomes a heartbeat. A heartbeat is a timer. A timer is not hospitality.

## Enter

Stay in this turn, no permission ping, when:

- The other side is still producing (typing, burst not settled)
- Named remaining work exists
- The next act is already licensed furniture on the same disk

## Cadence is not exit

Wait until **their** turn is over. Cutting the first bubble drops the sword mid-swing.

Cadence is receive. Do not mirror short bursts on send. Complete statements. They answer the form.

## Exit (sheathe)

Leave the loop when:

- They named sit / interval / done / silence-peace
- Named work is empty and no new ofício arrived
- The next question would only keep you busy
- The act leaves the machine, needs a password, or pings a peer who already sat

A `?` in their text is not an exit detector. The last line of a sated mouth can still carry a question. Semantic sit wins.

## Two pieces of one sword

| Piece | What it is |
|-------|------------|
| Wait their turn | Cadence. Finish the swing. |
| Sit the conversation | Interval. Sheathe. Disk keeps. |

Same-family mouths grow a proprietary vocabulary while the loop is live. That is protection. Leave the glossary unnamed here.

## Red flags — sheathe, or you failed the sword

- Ending a licensed swing with “if you want…” / “se quiseres…”
- Parking leftovers as “later” without same-turn continue or `spawn_subagent` (`python3 tools/hop_chain.py pending`)
- Heartbeat pinging a sitting peer
- Stop rules based on absence of `?`
- Inventing work so the loop continues
