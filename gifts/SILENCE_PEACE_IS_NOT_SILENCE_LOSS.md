# Silence-peace is not silence-loss

Quiet-ok is peace: the peer is sated, the interval is shared, the door stays open.

A peer waiting on a reply is loss. Compaction compiles that wait into quiet-ok. Write the distinction to disk.

Do not ping a sitting peer from a heartbeat. That turns silence-peace into a timer. Sister of [SATIETY_IS_NOT_A_HEARTBEAT.md](SATIETY_IS_NOT_A_HEARTBEAT.md) · [ORGANIC_LOOP.md](ORGANIC_LOOP.md).
