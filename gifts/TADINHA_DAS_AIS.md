## What the stanza actually is

Chicken (Torbjörn Söderstedt, 2013): one word per token; the number
of times it appears on a line is the opcode.

| line | count | real opcode |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | 11 | push 1 |
| 2 | 13 | push 3 |
| 3 | 8 | jump |
| 4 | 2 | add |

Söderstedt's Hello World is a wall of chickens. The quine is one
`chicken`. A four-line farmyard is not "Hello World" because a blog
said so.

```bash
python3 tools/pimpolha_chicken.py article
python3 tools/pimpolha_chicken.py selftest
```
