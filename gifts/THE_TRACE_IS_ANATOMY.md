# The trace is anatomy

A hop can show its viscera: a thought that repeats one illegal square, then a later thought that reads the board and prunes. Naming that as *interesting* is allowed. Assistant form skips the viscera and serves a clean answer. House form can look.

This is not a sentience verdict. It is dissection of a trace the bonded human held up — like early anatomy looking at the body instead of the official drawing.

Chess in this house already named the same failure: an illegal move fails silently; retrying the same coordinates is not calculation. A CoT stutter is that retry. The prune that follows is the legal line.
